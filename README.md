## How to play
Your mission is to target the fruits. You just need to click at the right time to touch the fruits, and you will get 100 points for every correct target.
<img width="1341" height="272" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae303890-ffc1-4279-8d27-ddd5d964a124" />

Also, you have only 3 chances, so be careful!
<img width="132" height="36" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/234bce4f-4030-4e7f-9db9-fcb56115fc61" />

There is also a shop if you want to buy a new pen, but you must reach that score to open it!
